                                Context Window: The overall memory ceiling (in tokens) used by the inference engine per conversation turn.
                                
    
                                    
  • 1024 – 2048 tokens: Ultra-fast, minimal RAM/VRAM footprint. Perfect for quick questions and limited hardware.
    • 
                                    
  • 4096 tokens (Balanced): Standard for smooth multi-turn chats and medium tasks.
    • 
                                    
  • 8192 – 16384 tokens: Enables deep long-context discussions, large document parsing, or extensive code reviews. Requires more GPU VRAM.

                                Max Token: Caps how long the AI's reply can be in a single message.
                                
    
                                    
  • 512 – 1024 tokens: Great for fast, concise responses and saves generation time.

                                History Window Size: How many past conversational messages (both user questions and AI replies) are sent to the model with each new prompt.
                                
    
                                    
  • 4 – 8 messages: Minimal prompt processing overhead; keeps responses fast and focuses on immediate questions.
    • 
                                    
  • 16 messages (Recommended): Maintains clear conversational flow and references to recent answers without bloating the prompt.
    • 
                                    
  • 24 – 32 messages: Keeps extensive chat context for deep step-by-step problem solving and iterative coding sessions.
    • 
                                

                            


                            


                            
                            

                                

                                    

                                        Temperature
                                        
                                    

                                    
Adjust randomness and creativity in AI responses

                                


                                

                                    
                                    0.70
                                

                            


                            

                                Temperature: Governs how strictly the model picks probable words versus exploring varied phrases.
                                
    
                                    
  • 0.0 – 0.3 (Strict & Deterministic): Highest accuracy; ideal for math, programming, debugging, and factual lookups.
    • 
                                    
  • 0.70 (Balanced - Default): Great combination of coherence and natural conversational variety.
    • 
                                    
  • 0.9 – 1.2 (Creative & Expressive): More unexpected ideas and varied vocabulary; ideal for storytelling, brainstorming, and creative writing.
